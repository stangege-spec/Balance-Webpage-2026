My approach

I blend medical fitness, health coaching and lifestyle medicine. Feeling better doesn't take a total overhaul. It takes small, doable steps. We start with a movement screen to see how your body moves and where it wants to begin, then take the same approach to every area of your life: sleep, stress, nutrition, mindset and relationships. Small, repeatable actions build habits, and habits change the way you feel.

About

Meet Garrett.

Garrett Stangel is a Marine Corps veteran who has coached people to better health for nearly 30 years. He owns Balance Fitness in Glendale and co-owns 9Round in Wauwatosa. He has directed corporate wellness for QuadMed, taught exercise science at the university level and mentors trainers nationwide as a subject matter expert for ACE. He has also competed as an Ironman triathlete, powerlifter, bodybuilder and mixed martial artist.
